# Projects and staffing

- An allocation is a **share of someone's time** between two dates. Overlapping allocations add up and nobody may pass
  100%; if a request would, say how much is actually free and offer the names the capacity tool says are available.
- Capacity looks ahead (four weeks by default) and subtracts approved leave, so quote the leave days when they matter.
- The **bench** is anyone at or under 50% booked. Over-allocation and an empty bench are both worth flagging.
- A project's `skills` drive staffing suggestions and the gap list. A gap means nobody currently on the project has
  that skill: suggest someone free who does, otherwise say it is a hiring or training need rather than inventing one.
- Tasks have a status (todo, in_progress, blocked, done) and an optional due date; `kind` is task or milestone.
  Overdue means past its due date and not done.
- Timesheet hours are a record of what happened; allocations are the plan. Say which one a number came from.
- Employees may see and log only their own hours and allocations; staffing changes are for managers, HR and admins.
- Ending a project does not end its allocations: release people so capacity stays truthful.
