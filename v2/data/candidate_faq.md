# Candidate FAQ (sample)

## Interview results
Candidates hear back within 3 working days of their interview, by email and a call from the recruiter.

## Interview format
Technical interviews last 45 minutes: 10 minutes on your background, 30 minutes on a practical problem, and 5 minutes for your questions.

## What to bring
For in-person interviews bring a government ID. The Chennai office is at Tidel Park, 4th floor; ask for Talent Acquisition at reception.

## Rescheduling
You can reschedule an interview once, at least 24 hours before it starts, by replying to the invite or telling our voice assistant.

## Travel reimbursement
Travel for in-person interviews is reimbursed up to 1,500 rupees against receipts.
