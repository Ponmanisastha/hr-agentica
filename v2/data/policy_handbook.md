# Employee Policy Handbook (sample)

## 1. Annual leave
Every full-time employee earns 18 days of annual leave per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per month. Annual leave must be applied for at least 7 calendar days in advance. Requests of up to 5 working days with enough balance and enough notice are approved automatically. Longer requests, or requests with short notice, need the manager's approval. Up to 6 unused days can be carried forward to the next year.

## 2. Sick leave
Employees get 8 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave needs no advance notice. Absences of more than 2 consecutive working days need a medical certificate, uploaded within 3 days of returning to work.

## 3. Casual leave
Employees get 6 days of casual leave per year for personal matters. Casual leave can be taken for at most 2 consecutive days and should be applied for at least 1 day in advance.

## 4. Public holidays
The company follows the published holiday calendar. Public holidays and weekends falling inside a leave period are not counted as leave days.

## 5. Maternity and paternity leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children, as per the Maternity Benefit Act. Paternity leave is 10 working days, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

## 6. Notice period and resignation
The notice period is 60 days for employees at level L3 and above, and 30 days for others. Unused annual leave can be set off against the notice period only with the manager's approval.

## 7. Work from home
Employees can work from home up to 2 days a week with their manager's agreement. Fully remote arrangements need HR approval.

## 8. Reimbursements
Travel and internet expenses are reimbursed against receipts submitted within 30 days. Internet reimbursement is capped at 1,000 rupees per month.

## 9. Onboarding documents
Every new hire must submit: signed offer letter, government ID proof, PAN card, address proof, bank account details, education certificates, signed NDA, and the relieving letter from the previous employer (if any). Payroll cannot be set up without bank details and PAN card.
