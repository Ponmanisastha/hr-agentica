# Work From Home Policy

Flexible work at Acme: when you can work from home and what is expected.

## 1. Work from home eligibility

Employees can work from home up to 2 days a week with their manager's agreement. Fully remote arrangements need HR approval and are reviewed every 6 months. Employees on probation work from the office for their first month.

## 2. Expectations when working from home

Be reachable during core hours, keep your calendar current, and join meetings with video where the team expects it. Work from a safe, private space and lock your screen when you step away.

## 3. Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a headset. A monitor can be issued on request. Internet costs are covered by the travel and expense policy.
