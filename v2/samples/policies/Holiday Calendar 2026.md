# Holiday Calendar 2026

Sample calendar for the Chennai office. Replace it with your own locations' calendars.

## Public holidays 2026

- 1 January, Thursday: New Year's Day
- 15 January, Thursday: Pongal
- 26 January, Monday: Republic Day
- 3 April, Friday: Good Friday
- 14 April, Tuesday: Tamil New Year
- 1 May, Friday: May Day
- 15 August, Saturday: Independence Day
- 14 September, Monday: Ganesh Chaturthi
- 2 October, Friday: Gandhi Jayanti
- 20 October, Tuesday: Ayudha Puja and Vijayadashami
- 8 November, Sunday: Deepavali
- 25 December, Friday: Christmas

## Optional holidays

Each employee may take 2 optional holidays a year from this list: Holi (4 March), Ramzan (21 March), Bakrid (27 May), Onam (26 August) and Guru Nanak Jayanti (24 November). Apply for them like casual leave; they do not reduce any leave balance.
