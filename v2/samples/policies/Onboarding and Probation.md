# Onboarding and Probation

How new joiners are brought on board and confirmed.

## 1. Onboarding documents

Every new hire must submit: signed offer letter, government ID proof, PAN card, address proof, bank account details, education certificates, signed NDA, and the relieving letter from the previous employer (if any). Payroll cannot be set up without bank details and PAN card.

## 2. First week

On day 1 the new hire gets a laptop, accounts and a buddy. IT sets up access before the joining date. The manager shares a 30-60-90 day plan in the first week. HR runs an induction on the policies, benefits and the code of conduct.

## 3. Probation

New employees are on probation for 6 months. The manager reviews progress at 3 months and confirms the employee at the end of probation, or extends probation once by up to 3 months. During probation the notice period is 15 days.
